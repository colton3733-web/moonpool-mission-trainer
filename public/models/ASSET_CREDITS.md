`kenney/model-bounds.json` records each model's world-space bounds and mesh count, measured with Three.js GLTFLoader. Models are Y-up and have a nonzero source translation near X=2, Z=1.5. Center the X/Z bounds and subtract minimum Y when placing them. Native heights range from 0.25 to 0.8 units; scale to the desired equipment size.
